Classic Carbonara Recipe

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook 400 grams of spaghetti until al dente, usually around nine minutes. Reserve one cup of pasta cooking water before draining.

While the pasta cooks, dice 150 grams of guanciale or pancetta and render it slowly in a cold pan until golden and crisp. Remove from heat. In a separate bowl, whisk together four large egg yolks, one whole egg, and 80 grams of finely grated Pecorino Romano. Add plenty of freshly cracked black pepper.

Add the drained hot pasta to the pan with the rendered pork. Off the heat, pour in the egg mixture and toss vigorously, adding splashes of reserved pasta water until you get a creamy, glossy sauce that coats every strand. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper. Never add cream — true carbonara gets its silkiness from the emulsified eggs alone.
